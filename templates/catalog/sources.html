Why there may be no lowest-price badge. A retailer's delivery advertisement does not confirm that a particular price and stock level apply to your address. We highlight a lowest listed price only when fresh, in-stock observations from at least two retailers have evidence for the same delivery area. The current public-page imports have no verified area. A lower observed number is still shown; delivery fees and checkout totals are excluded.

What a price means here

A collected price is an observation from a retailer, with a check time and delivery area when known. It isn't a final checkout quote. We show stale observations, but exclude them from the lowest-price highlight after {{ stale_hours }} hours under the current configuration.

How we collect

Searches use our local database. Collection happens separately and is disabled until each source's access rules and data format are validated. We don't bypass access challenges or location requirements.

What we compare

Brand, size, pack count, and variants are checked before fuzzy title matching. Ambiguous products stay separate. Different delivery areas aren't assigned a winning retailer.

Where you shop

DaamRadar doesn't take payments or arrange delivery. For verified collected listings, product links take you to the retailer to confirm the price and purchase.
